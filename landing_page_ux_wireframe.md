# AlgoZenith Landing Page — UI/UX Layout Breakdown

The landing page (homepage) needs to follow a specific visual hierarchy. When a 1st-year student opens the link on their phone, they should immediately know **what the club is**, **what is happening right now**, and **where to click**.

Here is the exact top-to-bottom layout you should build:

## 1. The Sticky Top Banner (The "FOMO" Driver)

**Placement:** Fixed at the very top of the screen.
**Purpose:** Drives immediate traffic to your current active event.

* **Visual:** A contrasting background color (e.g., bright orange or blue) with a pulsing red "Live" dot.

* **Copy:** `🔴 Registrations Open: 7-Day Java & Logic Building Bootcamp!`

* **Action:** A small `Register Now →` button.

## 2. Navigation Bar

**Placement:** Below the banner, sticky on scroll.

* **Left:** Club Logo + "AlgoZenith"

* **Right Links:** `Bootcamp` (Highlighted), `Events`, `Resources`, `Leaderboard`

* **CTA:** `Join Discord` button.

## 3. The Hero Section (The "Above-the-Fold" Hook)

**Placement:** The main screen area before the user scrolls down.
**Purpose:** Answers "Why should I join this club?" in 3 seconds.

* **Main Headline (H1):** *"Master DSA. Crack Hackathons. Land Tier-1 Placements."*

* **Sub-Headline (H2):** *"The official student developer and competitive programming chapter of \[College Name\]. From your first line of code to your final interview."*

* **Primary Button:** `Register for Day 1 Bootcamp` (Solid brand color, large).

* **Secondary Button:** `Explore Club Resources` (Outline style).

## 4. The "Trust & Scale" Stats Bar

**Placement:** Right below the hero section.
**Purpose:** Proves to juniors and college admin that the club is active and successful.

* **Layout:** A horizontal row of 4 big numbers.

  * `500+` Active Members

  * `120+` Bootcamp Registrations

  * `10+`  Events Hosted

## 5. Active Event Spotlight Card (Crucial for the Bootcamp)

**Placement:** Center of the page, high visibility.
**Purpose:** Gives all the details of the ongoing 7-Day Bootcamp without making them navigate away.

* **Visual:** A large, modern card with a dark gradient.

* **Content:**

  * Event Title: "7-Day DSA Bootcamp for Juniors"

  * Dates: "Oct 22 - Oct 28" | Venue: "Online + Offline Lab"

  * Countdown Timer: `[ 02 Days : 14 Hours : 30 Mins ]`

  * Action: `RSVP `

## 6. Our Core Domains

**Placement:** Grid layout below the event spotlight.
**Purpose:** Shows that the club is about more than just one thing.

* **Card 1:** 🏆 **Competitive Programming** (Weekly contests, LeetCode sheets)

* **Card 2:** 🌐 **Web & App Dev** (Open-source, hackathons, React/Node)

## 7. The Faces Behind AlgoZenith (Core Team)

**Placement:** Just above the footer.
**Purpose:** Humanizes the club so juniors know exactly who to approach for mentorship or doubts.

* **Headline:** "Meet the Core Team"
* **Visual:** A clean, responsive grid (e.g., 3-4 columns on desktop, 2 on mobile) of profile cards.
* **Card Content:**
  * Clean Headshot
  * Name & Current Academic Year (e.g., "Rahul Sharma, 3rd Year")
  * Club Role (e.g., "President" or "DSA Lead")
  * Small clickable icons: LinkedIn, GitHub, and Codeforces/LeetCode

## 8. Footer

**Placement:** Bottom of the page.

* **Social Links:** Instagram, LinkedIn, GitHub, Discord.

* **Quick Links:** Code of Conduct, Contact Core Team, Sponsor Deck.

* **Credits:** "Designed & Built with ❤️ by the AlgoZenith Tech Team."
